What We Offer

Development Programmes

Our programmes are designed to help women at every stage — whether you're exploring an idea, running a home business, or looking to scale.

  • Entrepreneurship Development

    Start, plan and manage your own business from scratch.

  • Business Training

    Practical skills for running a business day to day.

  • Accounts & Financial Literacy

    Understand cash flow, pricing, budgets and record-keeping.

  • Digital Marketing

    Promote your business on social media, WhatsApp and online.

  • AI for Business

    Learn how AI tools can help with content, planning and customer service.

  • Branding & Packaging

    Make your product stand out with better branding and presentation.

  • Product Development

    Improve your products and develop new ones for the market.

  • Sales Training

    Learn to sell with confidence — in person, online and through networks.

  • E-commerce

    Set up and manage online sales channels for your products.

  • Export Readiness

    Understand what it takes to sell beyond your local market.

  • Leadership Development

    Build leadership, communication and management skills.

  • Skill Development

    Hands-on workshops in crafts, food, textiles and other trades.
