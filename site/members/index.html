Membership Value

Why Become a Member?

  • Business Networking

    Connect with fellow entrepreneurs and business contacts.

  • Market Access

    Sell through FEMME Stalls, exhibitions and partner channels.

  • FEMME Stalls

    Priority access and preferential rates for stall registration.

  • Training Programmes

    Skills development in business, digital, finance and more.

  • Mentoring

    Learn from experienced entrepreneurs and business leaders.

  • Business Collaborations

    Find partners, distributors and joint venture opportunities.

  • Chennai Connections

    Access to Chennai-based networks, markets and opportunities.

  • NRI Networking

    Connect with overseas community members and opportunities.

  • Finance Information

    Learn about government schemes, bank loans and funding options.

  • Joint Ventures

    Partner with other members for larger business opportunities.

  • Recognition

    Showcase your achievements and get visibility within the community.

  • Leadership

    Opportunities to take leadership roles within Femme Stalls.
