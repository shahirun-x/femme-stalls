    The journey

    From a single stall to self-reliance.

    Every member's path looks different, but the shape is the same. It starts with simply showing up — and it grows, step by step, into a business and a life built on your own terms.

    1. 01

      Participation

      Join the community, take a stall, meet women walking the same road.

    2. 02

      Confidence

      Stand behind your own products and find your voice with customers.

    3. 03

      Skills

      Learn pricing, marketing, accounts and the craft of running a business.

    4. 04

      Income

      Turn skill into steady earnings — at stalls, online and through the network.

    5. 05

      Self-reliance

      Stand on your own, then reach back and lift the next woman up.
