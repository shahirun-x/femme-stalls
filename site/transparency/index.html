Structure

Governance Structure

  1. General Body

    All registered members of Femme Stalls.

  2. Elected by members

  3. Executive Committee

    President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer and Coordinators.

  4. Managed by EC

  5. Operations

    Events, Programmes, Membership and Communications.

The Executive Committee is elected by members and serves a defined term.

Open Books

Financial Transparency

We believe in keeping our finances open. Here's a summary of how funds are managed.

Income Sources

  • Membership fees (if applicable)
  • Stall registration fees
  • Sponsorship
  • Partner contributions
  • Programme fees

Expenditure Areas

  • Event organisation
  • Training & programmes
  • Communications & promotion
  • Administration
  • Community activities

Reporting

  • Annual financial summaries are shared with members at the Annual General Meeting.
  • Event-specific income and expenditure reports are available for major events.
  • Members can request financial information through the Executive Committee.

Detailed financial reports will be published here as they become available.
